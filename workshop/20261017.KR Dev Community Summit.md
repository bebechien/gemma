```

All characters and objects should look handcrafted out of matte modeling clay with visible fingerprint dents, sculpted seam lines, and slight surface texture imperfections. Render at an authentic stepped 12 fps feel while matching the real-time subject movement and camera path. No glossy plastic shading.
```

```
